## Using a Template

To use a template, click on the "Templates" button in the sign overview menu.
This will open the template selector, where you can browse through all available templates.

### Resource Templates

Resource templates are templates that are included in a sign pack, or by default in ClickSigns.
You can browse through the available resource templates, click on one to select it,
and click "Apply Template" to apply it to your sign.

### Local Templates

Local templates are templates that you saved to your local templates folder (`.minecraft/sign_templates`).
You can browse through the available local templates, click on one to select it,
and click "Apply Template" to apply it to your sign.

### Creating a Local Template

#### Open the Sign Editor [step]
To create a local template, first, open the sign editor for the sign you want to save as a template.

#### Export as Template [step]
Then, inside the editor, click on "Export Template".

> Here, you can check **Include Written Texts** if you want the template
to include the text that you have written on the sign.
Otherwise, all text elements will have empty text.

You can also change the name and author of the template here.

#### Save to Local Templates [step]
Click on "Save to Local Templates" to save the template to your local templates.

#### Done! [step]
You can now find your template in the "Local Templates" tab of the template selector!

### Sharing a Local Template
To share a local template, you can send someone the template json file
from your local templates folder (`.minecraft/sign_templates`).

The recipient can then copy the template json file into their own local templates folder,
and it will show up in the "Local Templates" tab of the template selector for them to use.
